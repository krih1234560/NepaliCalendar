Nepali Calendar and नेपाली पात्रो

Nepali Calendar is a free online नेपाली पात्रो for viewing Bikram Sambat dates, English dates, festivals, holidays and converting BS dates to AD.

The calendar supports a wide range of Bikram Sambat dates and uses year-specific calendar data rather than assuming that every Nepali month has the same number of days.
