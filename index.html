About Nepali Calendar and नेपाली पात्रो

This Nepali Calendar is designed for people looking for Nepali date today, नेपाली पात्रो, Bikram Sambat dates, BS to AD conversion, Nepali festivals and public holidays. The calendar uses year-specific Bikram Sambat month data rather than assuming every Nepali month has the same number of days.

Holiday information can change by government notice. For official public-holiday decisions, always cross-check the latest notice from the Government of Nepal, Ministry of Home Affairs.
